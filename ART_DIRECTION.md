# Cloudkeepers — first visual chapter

Warm storybook miniature, tactile foliage, terracotta rock, cream and peach clouds, sage green, amber gold. The little explorer is a provisional concept; the child co-designer owns the final character description.

## Generated assets

Built-in `image_gen.imagegen` was used for all three original raster assets. Local WebP copies preserve character transparency and make the entire site independent of external image hosts. The source PNGs remain in the Codex generated-image library.

A lush grassy floating island occupies the central and lower half of frame, in a sea of huge soft peach cream white clouds under a mint turquoise blue sky. Isometric three-quarter view looking down enough to see a broad open grassy oval clearing at the island center, where a separately drawn character will walk. Big central island spans x 23% to 80% and y 35% to 88%. Warm golden morning light, soft ambient occlusion, vivid jade foliage, terracotta pink exposed layered rock underneath, hanging vines, one very thin waterfall spilling off the far right edge. A winding sandy path around the clearing, clusters of tiny daisies and peach tulips and oversized leafy plants, three round stylized trees toward back left, a small charming terracotta roof woodland cottage with round window at back center-right, tiny timber signpost at path left. In background a smaller distant floating garden island in upper right, another tiny distant island in left midground, atmospheric depth. At the far right edge of main island a tiny wooden dock hints at future island hopping. Composition: leave generous calm uncluttered sky in top 25% and upper left third for UI overlay; foreground clouds along lower edge. Main island clearing must remain completely empty and walkable looking. The vibe is magical safe curious bright, premium polished indie family game art, tactile dimensional miniature, crisp details not overly busy. No text, no letters, no logos, no numbers, no UI, no characters, no animals, no balloon or vehicles. Do not draw any player characters; environment only.

## Code assets and typography

The sky ferry, cloud logo, and UI symbols are original inline SVGs. Future island and animal thumbnails are clearly labelled concept ideas, with open spaces for the family's designs; they are not twelve completed levels.

Nunito is bundled locally as `assets/nunito.woff2`. Its SIL Open Font License is preserved in `assets/Nunito-OFL.txt`. Main titles use the system Georgia serif.
